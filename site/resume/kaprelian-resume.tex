\section{Experience}

\entry{Ministry of Economy of the Republic of Armenia}{Sep 2020 -- Jul 2022}{Economic Research Assistant, Economic Research Center}{Yerevan, Armenia}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Co-authored the Ministry's quarterly \textit{Armenian Economic Outlook} report used by Fitch Ratings to help evaluate Armenia's sovereign credit rating.
  \item Estimated trade effects of the Turkey embargo over $\sim$1{,}000 6-digit HS-code lines (UN Comtrade, 2018--2021), identifying import substitution and 26\% substitute-supplier price inflation (vs.\ $\sim$6\% globally), and forecasting outcomes of removal.
  \item Estimated Armenia's incremental capital-output ratio ($\sim$2.3) and a $\sim$\$1B national capital-investment gap benchmarked against 11 peer economies, informing policymaker guidance.
  \item Modeled the fiscal effects of a proposed 6\% payroll-tax health-insurance mandate via domestic consumption functions and shadow-economy dynamics, projecting an annual budget cost near 4\% of GDP.
  \item Evaluated a VAT-reduction proposal to formalize the shadow economy, recommending alternatives after finding only 40--60\% consumer pass-through and asymmetric price response.
\end{itemize}
