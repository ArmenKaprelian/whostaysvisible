# Figure 2.8 restyle: ranking and reasoning

Status: review artifact for the author, 18 October 2026. Nothing here changes the manuscript or authorizes a change; adoption goes through Codex with a numbered before/after comparison. Outputs: `figures/20261018-fig28/` (PDFs, previews, `fig28-all-variants.pdf`), `gallery/fig28.html` (self-contained comparison page), `data/fig28/` (classes, cutpoints, palettes, validation, with provenance and checks).

## What the figure is, and why it reads flat

Figure 2.8 (`chapters/02-opacity/07-financial-sample-implications.tex`, `figures/chapter2/stage3-08-cohort-map.pdf`) colours 100 km cells by the share of located eligible 2021 firms that belong to each revenue cohort, on a continuous 101-step navy ramp with a linear 0–100% legend. Supported cells span 8–68% (cash) and 5–58% (inventory), so the data use the middle third of the ramp. The colours at the five fifths of cells sit 0.02–0.05 apart in OKLab lightness, below the 0.06 step a reader can see. Nothing about the data is wrong; the scale wastes its range.

The "established quintiles" of the project are firm asset quintiles (Figure 2.5). They group firms, not cells, so they cannot colour this map without changing its variable. The author chose instead to class the cells at pooled type-7 quintiles of the inclusion share over the 1,334 supported cells of both panels: cutpoints 33.3 / 38.3 / 43.0 / 47.6%, one legend for both panels. Pooling keeps the caption's common scale; it also means the cash panel sits mostly in the top two classes (186 + 222 of 667 cells) and the inventory panel in the bottom two (197 + 207), which is the true cross-panel difference.

## Direction

1. **Classing does the work; the shape is secondary.** The control render (25 km gaps with the current continuous ramp, `s1b-gap25-continuous`) still reads as one flat blue. Every classed variant, whatever the shape, separates five groups at a glance.
2. **Recommended: tiles with 25 km gaps and the classed navy (`s1b-gap25-c1`).** The gap is 1.0 pt on the page, which reads as a crisp mosaic at print size without looking sparse; each cell keeps 56% of its area in colour, so the five classes are the easiest to tell apart of all shapes; the land outline shows through the gaps; and the change to the production function is three lines (half-side, palette, legend).
3. **Second: fixed circles (`s2-dots-c1`).** The Hunt Brothers aesthetic, with the gray unsupported cells as small recessive dots that show the sampling footprint. Two costs: a 2.8 pt dot carries 38% of the cell's area, so the two darkest classes are harder to separate at page scale; and a dot grid reads as point locations (registered addresses) rather than the equal-area cells the text describes.
4. **Third: graduated circles (`s3-graduated-c1`).** Size and colour both encode the class, so the hierarchy is the strongest of all and the grayscale version is the most legible. The same double encoding exaggerates the dense core against the periphery, and the smallest class dots (45 km) look like the gray unsupported dots (35 km) at a glance. Worth keeping in mind for a grayscale-only print.
5. **Colour, option 1: the classed navy (`C1`).** `#78A3C9 #4F84B4 #2F6395 #16436E #061C38`, anchored on the manuscript's navy `#16436E` and the ramp's dark end. Validated against the land colour: monotone, every adjacent lightness step at least 0.102 (two to five times the current steps), light end 2.37:1, one hue. It keeps the navy identity of Figure 2.7, the "shared palette for the same quantity" rule, and puts the visual weight on high coverage, which is what the prose discusses.
6. **Colour, option 2: the classed multi-hue ramp (`C2`).** ColorBrewer YlGnBu 9-class steps 5–9, `#41B6C4 #1D91C0 #225EA8 #253494 #081D58`: monotone, steps at least 0.099, light end 2.14:1, colour-vision safe. The hue change adds separation at the light end, but the bright teal draws the eye to the low-coverage cells and the three dark steps drift toward indigo, away from the house navy. Choose it only if the extra pop matters more than consistency with the rest of Chapter 2.
7. **Dark2: not usable as the scheme, shown for comparison (`s1b-gap25-dark2`, `s2-dots-dark2`).** Quintiles are ordered and Dark2 is qualitative, so "darker = higher" disappears and every patch needs a legend lookup. The validator in map (all-pairs) mode fails it twice: pink `#E7298A` against teal `#1B9E77` differ by 1.7 under deuteranopia, and lime `#66A61E` against teal by 10.5 even with full colour vision (floor 15); adjacent lightness steps are 0.005–0.05, so the grayscale crop is one gray. The classed navy delivers the discreteness the author wanted without those costs.
8. **Optional two-hue version (`e-gap25-navy-green`).** Navy classes for cash and a lightness-matched phthalo green (`#6BAD9A #36917A #00715B #004D3D #00231A`, hue of `#004B3C`) for inventory. It follows the manuscript's rule for adjacent cohort displays, but both panels show the same quantity and the prose compares them, so the shared palette is the rule-conforming choice. Keep it only if the author wants the cohorts read as separate displays.

## Details

- Gap width: 25 km (1.0 pt) over 15 km (0.6 pt); the thinner gap is 1.8 px at 220 dpi and the map reads nearly contiguous again at page scale (`s1a-gap15-c1`).
- Dots: diameter 70% of the pitch (radius 35 km); unsupported cells at 40% (radius 20 km) in the production gray. Graduated: diameters 45, 57.5, 70, 82.5 and 95% of the pitch by class.
- Legend: six swatches on the footprint of the current bar (squares for tiles, circles for dots, two rows for the two-hue version) with right-closed class ranges "5–33%", ">33–38%", ">38–43%", ">43–48%", ">48–68%" and "<50 eligible", at 10.1 pt (new text meets the lab's 10 pt floor). Note line 2 becomes "Classes: pooled quintiles of supported cells; 100 km cells; gray: <50 eligible; overlapping cohorts. RFSD v3.1.0." Every other string is verbatim, including the stray "02 " prefix in the first title.
- Pooled classes per panel: cash 70 / 61 / 128 / 186 / 222 cells, inventory 197 / 207 / 137 / 81 / 45. Per-panel quintiles (not chosen; recorded in `data/fig28/fig28-cutpoints.csv`) would give 133–134 cells per class and two legends.
- Grayscale (`*-zoom-gray.png`): the current figure is a mush of mid-grays; C1 and C2 keep five distinguishable grays; Dark2 collapses.
- Fidelity: the production function and the lab's own draw function both reproduce the manuscript PDF with zero differing pixels at 200 dpi and identical text and fonts (`figures/20261018-fig28/fidelity/fidelity.txt`), so every difference in the variants is the encoding alone.
- `scripts/pdf-check.sh`: all PDFs pass geometry (468 × 504 pt) and NimbusSans; the smallest word is 7.80 pt in every file, inherited from the manuscript figure (its notes are drawn at 0.78 × 10.5 pt).
