# Armen G. Kaprelian: design preferences and principles

Written in the dataviz lab on 25 October 2026 from the records of rounds 1 to 11 of the motion study: the plan file (`~/.claude/plans/can-you-within-your-elegant-lantern.md`), the round sections of `motion/README.md`, the decision log of the lab `README.md`, the memory, and the author's corrections at each checkpoint. Every principle below is paired with the decision that produced it and, where one exists, the option rejected and why. Dates are 2026. "The author" is Armen G. Kaprelian; the lab is Claude's.

## Summary (one page)

The work is a dark, flat, monospace system in which colour means one thing at a time and nothing on screen is decoration.

- **Identity.** One mark (four corner brackets and a dot), one typeface (IBM Plex Mono at 400, 500 and 700), capitals for names and interface labels with 8 percent tracking, the striped display style on large type only. Symbols are never striped. (Round 10, 25 October.)
- **Colour.** A near-black navy stage with the land as a lit shape on it; two accents with fixed roles, fuchsia where a control acts on the map and neon green where a panel only reads, both at full strength at rest and brighter only while something moves or has focus; one exception, the ice blue of the GO TO card, because travel neither acts on the data nor reads it; the listed red reserved for firms on the five lists and the one red control that belongs to them; a single-hue cyan ramp for ordered quantities; one amber and cyan pair for signed change; nine lit industry hues validated pairwise; the accents never on data, ramps or the red. (Rounds 9 and 10.)
- **Motion.** Motion reveals data or moves the camera, nothing else. Camera moves are pure functions of time and zoom toward their subject. The pulse is the one glow. Under reduced motion everything jumps, holds or crossfades, and no loop runs. The wire scrolls on through context changes; only a year change rebuilds it, under a crossfade. (Rounds 1, 6, 8, 9, 11.)
- **Text.** Three tones: ink #ECEEF1, muted #B4C0D0, dim #7D889C. Descriptive wording, the chapter's own words, no em-dashes, no semicolons, every rate with its denominator; units keep their case inside tracked capitals. The muted tone was lifted one step on 25 October so that 12 px provenance reads on the panel. (Rounds 3, 9, 10.)
- **Layout.** Borders by role, squared corners everywhere but the round listed-state glyphs, a fine grain on every chrome surface and never on the map, one collapse convention (a square toggle, remembered per viewer), the header as three groups that never touch, the phone as a scrolling page with stacked cards. (Rounds 6, 10, 11.)
- **Data honesty.** Every figure on screen comes from a checked file and can be re-derived; the wording is descriptive, never causal; identity is never carried by colour alone; the audit at five widths on both renderers must report zero findings before anything is published, and the author's word precedes every publish. (Every round.)
- **Rejected.** A left-edge bar on buttons; muted accents at rest; dragging windows; the Syne title face; a third accent; white body text; a news feed in the wire.

## 1. Identity

**The mark.** Four L-shaped corner brackets of arm 6 and stroke 2 in a 24-unit square with a dot of radius 2.5 at the centre, drawn in the current colour (the author's item 14b, 25 October). Where it appears, and nowhere else: before the explainer's page title, in the video credits before the name line, as the focus corners on every button and toggle of both pages, as the minimap's Russia frame, as the wire's separator (14 px with 2 px strokes since round 11), as the year slider's thumb (20 px, 2.5 px strokes, a 4 px dot, in the neon green), at 32 px in the header lock-up beside the name (round 11, item 8), and with the initials inside its square at 4 percent under every data layer of the map and the video stage (item 17). Never on the map at a data location, where it would read as a marker. The atlas title lost its mark on 25 October (round 11) when the lock-up took it.

**One typeface.** IBM Plex Mono at 400 for prose, 500 for labels and figures, 700 for headings, years and counters; tabular figures throughout; installed locally from the pinned files for the headless renders and served to the published pages from Google Fonts with the local family first in the stack (round 10, T1). Decision: the author asked for one typeface everywhere. Rejected: the Syne display face for titles, offered in the plan as an experiment with a title-face flag; dropped by the author on 25 October ("one typeface, no flag, no pin, no install"). Rejected earlier: Nimbus Sans with Arimo as the web fallback (rounds 1 to 9), replaced because a monospace sets every figure tabular by construction and the labels read as one system.

**The capitals rule.** Proper names first (the page title, the scene titles, the hook, the credits title, the place names on the map and in the location, region and strip lines), then every interface label (buttons, the year label and value, the legend title and rows, the panel row labels, the tips, the mix title, the minimap labels), in Plex Mono 500 with 8 percent tracking (round 10, T4, widened by the author on 25 October). Mixed case stays only for running prose, firm names, legal-form tags and unit symbols: a unit keeps its case inside a tracked label through a span, since "M" or "KM" read as other symbols. The DOM chapter buttons of the explainer are navigation, not titles, and keep mixed case. Rejected: synthesised small capitals (Plex Mono has no small-cap glyphs and synthesised ones break the monospace rhythm), so "small capitals" is set as tracked capitals at the small size.

**The striped display type and its exclusions.** Large type is cut by horizontal hairline gaps: eight bars per capital height on canvas (1 px gaps on the atlas, 2 px in the video), a 3 px pitch on the page (a fractional pitch blurs under the mask's antialiasing, a recorded deviation). Where: VISIBILITY ATLAS and the explainer's title line, the scene titles and the hook, the large year in the video's location panel; the legend's ramp bars are the one striped non-text element (round 10, S18). Excluded, by the author's correction of 25 October: the mark, the listed-state glyphs, the pulse button's ring and dot, the region glyphs, the minimap's dots and frame and any future symbol are drawn whole; the year value is set at 16 px, under the display floor, unstriped. The rule: stripes belong to display type and to buttons' text at display size, never to a symbol.

## 2. Colour

**The ground.** The stage is a near-black navy, #060B18 (OKLab L 0.152), and the land a lit shape on it, #172A45 (L 0.284, 1.36:1 on the stage); the panels sit 0.026 above the stage in lightness, the grid and borders rescaled by the same rule, the graticule a whiter 1 px line #2E333D that competes with neither the outline nor the data (round 9, the author's decision after the first palette frames). Rejected: the charcoal ground of rounds 3 to 8 (#121418 and #22262D), chosen then from four Central Moscow renders over a navy field, a grey or cream ground and phthalo green; and the round-2 navy (#0B1A2E and #1E2E42), rejected in round 9 because the listed red fell to 2.9:1 on that land, under the 3:1 floor the quiet national marks rely on. The light theme keeps its own tokens and is never the default: the atlas opens dark on every device whatever the browser's scheme, the button still switches (round 10, C6); the explainer has one dark theme because its rasters and renders are made for the dark stage.

**Two accents and their roles.** Fuchsia (`--cursor` #FF45C8, live #FF8FDF) for everything that acts on the map or follows the cursor: the crosshair, the tile's border, the hover ring cased in the stage colour, the focus corners, the LAYER card and its buttons, the zoom box, the theme button. Neon green (`--context` #4DFF7F, live #9AFFB8) for everything that reads: the wire and its glyph, the tile footer's figures, the year value and the slider's mark, the Under the cursor and chart cards, the legend, the note, the mix box, the scale bar, the card and overlay titles, the METHOD button (the only green button). Both validated for AA on the stage, the land and the panel and for colour-vision distance from the listed red and from each other (fuchsia 22 normal, 25 protan, 21 deutan from the red; green 49, 48, 28; 51, 41, 24 between them). The rule, in the author's words: fuchsia you act on, green you read; two accents only. Rejected: muted resting variants (`--cursor-muted`, `--context-muted`), the lab's first proposal in round 9, corrected the same day: "neon means neon", the full colours rest and the live variants show only while something moves or has focus. Rejected: a third accent (the GO TO card first took the ink as a neutral, then a blue, never a third accent role).

**The ice exception for travel.** The GO TO card, its buttons and the minimap take a pale blue, #9CC8FF for the dots and strokes and the card's border, #CFE6FF for the leaders, with the pressed city in a solid fill and stage-coloured text (round 10, N21 and the minimap correction of 25 October). The reason, recorded with the exception: travel neither acts on the data nor reads it, so it gets the page's neutral; the legend and every other panel keep their role colours. The first neutral was the ink white; the author moved it to an ice blue on the white side (#CFE6FF), validated against the cyan ramp's pale end, the Manufacturing blue and both accents (no pale blue-white clears 15 from the ramp's pale end, so the author's value stood and the pair is recorded), then colder (#9CC8FF, 15.5 from the pale end). The selected preset shows in the full neon green until the camera leaves it; hover lights the route to the live green with the name kept in the ink; keyboard focus keeps the fuchsia frame. Round 14 (25 October 2026) extends the exception twice: the cursor tile's text takes the same ice (the author's request: "the same ice-blue as the border of the GO TO menu"), while the tile's footer keeps the reading green and the listed count its red; and the GO TO card's search by name and its link to the view are travel controls in the card's ice.

**The listed red.** #E31A1C for firms on the five lists and nothing else: solid with a statement, hollow without one that year, a thin rim before the listing year (round 7), quieter with the zoom (rounds 8 and 11: alpha 0.60 and a 0.85 px floor at the country, the halo gone there) and the bloom at the listing year. Round 14 (B″, the author's verdict that the marks were "a bit overemphasized" at the country and the city, "though I don't want them to disappear from view"): in the atlas the resting alpha 0.48 and the floor 0.70 px at the country, and a casing in the stage colour in place of the pale halo, rising from k 20 to 60; the video keeps round 11's marks. Rejected: drawing the marks as pixel squares like the field (variant C), which made them louder. The one red figure in text is the Cells tile's listed count, a bold 19 px number after the legend's dot (4.0:1 on the dark tile, large text). The one red control is the pulse button, "because it belongs to the red marks" (25 October); every other control takes a role colour. The red is never a class colour and the chrome never uses it. Rejected: a dark purple for "statement absent" marks of firms on no list (round 3, change 1): the state is still computed but not drawn.

**The cyan ramp.** Six steps at even OKLCH lightness 0.556 to 0.940 with the hue sliding from 195 to 172 degrees (#1C8383 to #C7F8E7), validated as an ordinal ramp on the land and the stage (round 10, C5). The accent is step 4, the accent-strong step 6, so every chart reads the same family; the light theme keeps the manuscript's navy ramp. Rejected: YlGnBu (rounds 2 to 9), a multi-hue ramp that failed the one-hue check by design; the author asked for a single hue on the dark theme.

**The amber and cyan pair.** Declines in amber to orange (#FFB000, #F39523, #EA7719, the inner step held at L 0.69 so it stays 10.3 ΔE from the listed red under deutan vision) and rises in the cyan ramp's steps 3 to 5, the zero class a slate (#6B7482), for the Change layer, both revenue layers and the explainer's change map (round 10, S10). The author's orange was approximated and the amber kept exact; the outer ends differ in lightness by 0.06 because the amber half is held down by the red, stated in the record. Rejected: PRGn purple and green (rounds 2 to 9), replaced so that the signed layers share one pair with the ramp's own family.

**The lit industry nine.** The author's nine hues moved inside their semantic windows by the validator's coordinate search so that every pair among the nine, the neutral and the red is at least 13.9 normal and 8.5 colour-vision apart (Agriculture #E8B533, Manufacturing #2882F9, Construction #ED7D43, Trade #4CB3A4, Transport #FBEB87, Finance #E090FD, Professional #9887C7, Public #ECF4FE, Other #6D747A); the residual pairs under the floors are recorded with the legend and the tile naming the class as the secondary encoding (round 10, C7). Rejected: ColorBrewer qualitative sets (round 9's first nine) and two levers the lab offered to meet the floors (drawing the Firms view's no-revenue pairs in the "below" tone, letting Transport go paler), not applied.

**What never takes colour.** The chrome's neutrals: the map wrap's 1 px border, the header and footer bands, the ghost names (a tone one step above the land, a cold tint since round 12), the hairline regional borders at the national zoom, the graticule, the grain. The accents never colour data, ramps or the red; the red never colours chrome; nothing on the map canvas is coloured by an interface role except the hovered region's fuchsia outline and the cursor ring, which follow the cursor. The region tint and the glow under the dot field were the field's own colours, pulled back twice by the author (8 and 6 percent) until they sat at the level of a visibility assist, "never a display"; round 14 retired both with the pixel field, which follows the video's slide and has neither.

## 3. Motion

**What moves, and when.** The camera (flights through one easing with cubic position and log-k zoom; every move zooms toward its subject, the destination converging on the centre from the first frame, the author's choice of 24 October over a pan-then-zoom), the bloom of newly listed firms (a flash, a glow disc and two rings, staggered west to east, once per forward year change and on the pulse button), the year crossfades (presentation, never interpolation of values), the wire's scroll at 60 px per second, the strip's crossfade and the ticking figures of the tile footer, the minimap's leaders drawing on once at load, the slider thumb's brackets opening 2 px on hover and drag, the chart lines' reveals; since round 14 the Firms view's opening sweep (once per browser tab, the first time the view opens at the country view, each pixel and listed mark fading in as an invisible edge passes west to east over 1.3 s; any input completes it; never under reduced motion), the year dissolve (0.25 s, a crossfade, kept under reduced motion), the Cells view's pulse (the same two rings at the centre of each cell holding a firm listed that year) and the pulse of a firm found by name. The principle (round 1, held since): motion only reveals data or moves the camera; discrete labelled annual snapshots; camera moves are pure functions of time so a frame is reproducible. Rejected: window dragging for the three map overlays (25 October: "No window dragging"; the mix box, the note and the legend read, none acts).

**The reduced-motion rule.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` flights jump, the pulse is a still highlight held for the same 1.8 s, the wire does not scroll and its items crossfade one at a time, the leaders do not draw on, the brackets do not open, the strip swaps without transition; no loop runs. Every motion has a still form that carries the same information.

**The pulse as the one glow.** The flat-colour rule of round 10 (T3c): no shadow, gradient, filter, blur or backdrop effect anywhere on either page; what stays is translucency (the atlas panels at 92 percent, the scale bar at 85, the video's panels at 0.92 and the close panel at 0.6), and the only glow is the pulse's disc. The glow under the dot field at the national zoom (a blurred copy of the field at 6 percent, a visibility assist) was retired in round 14 with the pixel field.

**The wire's continuity.** The context strip of round 9 became a data wire of short dated true statements in round 10 and, in round 11, a queue that scrolls on through every context change: a change of region, layer or view appends the new cycle after the items queued and replaces only sets that have not entered the view; a year change rebuilds under a 0.3 s crossfade, since every item names the year. The principle: a running line never jumps because the viewer moved the cursor. Rejected: the CSS keyframe loop of round 10, which rebuilt and restarted the track at every change.

## 4. Text

**The three tones.** Ink #ECEEF1 (12.4:1 on the stage) for text that carries the content, muted #B4C0D0 for the second voice (sentence lines of legends, provenance in panels, the dissertation title), dim #7D889C for the smallest provenance and the footer. The muted tone was lifted one step on 25 October (from #9EABBD; the light theme's #52636D by the same OKLab step), the ink and the dim kept; the contrast table was re-run and the legend note and the METHOD block shown before and after. Rejected: pure white for body text. The ink has been #ECEEF1 since the charcoal palette of round 3; pure white appears only as a live variant (the GO TO card's hover under the ink-neutral proposal, superseded by the ice blue), never as a resting tone.

**The wording standard.** The chapter's own vocabulary: a statement is available or absent, a firm is removed from the registry, "listed" and "firms on no list"; "designation" only for the sanctions file's dated act; "(treated)" and "(comparison group)" as atlas-only glosses defined once on the page (24 October; "(designated)" declined because four of the five lists are undated memberships); never "supplied list", "treatment group", "sanctioned or strategic" or "control" on screen (the credits' caveat "Supplied lists are fixed memberships, not dated designations." is the recorded exception where provenance belongs); geocodes are house-level, street-level and other; pile counts are "distinct coordinate pairs"; shared registrations are explained in the author's three sentences with "can", not "often". Mechanics: no em-dashes and no semicolons on screen, one decimal place for percentages, named years, active voice with neutral subjects, every rate with its denominator, one message per scene in the title line, caveats once where they first matter. Firms on the five lists are named from the list files (25 October), firms on no list never, and the neighbour firm stays anonymous.

**Additions of 25 October 2026 (round 13, the author's decisions).** A fact that carries two statements is two sentences, never one joined by a semicolon (the wire's facts, the fine-cell line). Wherever the strip, the tile or the wire names a division, it is the short label with the code, "Wholesale trade (46)", never the code alone; the full NACE title stays in the panel and the METHOD block. The strip's slot carries the essentials only, the region's name and the year in tracked capitals, and the facts ride the wire. Firm names stay as filed. The capitals in the wire stay.

**Units.** Lower case inside tracked capitals through a span, joined to their number with a non-breaking space ("(250 m)", "100 km cells", "20 km"); unit symbols are fixed forms.

**Capitals.** As in section 1: names and labels in tracked capitals, prose in mixed case; a place name is the only part of a fact sentence set in capitals.

## 5. Layout

**Role borders.** Every button carries a 3 px border in its role colour at rest and the live variant on hover, the pressed state the solid accent fill with the stage colour as text, disabled at 0.4; the side column's cards and the explainer's control bar 3 px; the map overlays (the legend, the tile, the mix box, the wire's top rule, the scale bar, the zoom box's frame) 2 px so the map stays quiet; the map wrap a 1 px neutral; the toggles take their parent's role (round 10, H11, corrected twice from the mock). Rejected: a 3 px left-edge bar inside a 1.5 px border as the role marker, the lab's proposal and the mock of 25 October, removed by the author "on any button or control, anywhere, permanently". Rejected earlier: a clipped corner, because `clip-path` clips the border and the focus outline and a pressed fill loses a corner against the card's square frame.

**Squared corners.** Zero radius on every button, panel, card, swatch, tooltip and the map wrap, the explainer's controls included; the one exception, with its reason: the listed-state glyphs and the minimap's dots stay round because they depict the round marks on the map.

**The grain.** One 128 px thresholded noise tile as a second background layer on every chrome surface (the header and footer bands, the cards, the legend, the tile, the mix box, the wire, the scale bar, the zoom box, the explainer's controls, header and footer), never on the map canvas and never in the rendered video; its lift and drop are equal (about 6 levels on the dark theme, 4 on the light) so the measured contrast of text on a grained patch stays within 0.05 of the plain surface, which the audit guards (round 10, G22). Rejected: the first overlay-blend tile at 2.5 percent, invisible on the dark panel (sub-LSB).

**The collapse convention.** One square toggle (26 by 24 px, the parent's role colour, the glyph in the neon green, the fuchsia focus corners, `aria-expanded`) at the top right of every title row: the legend, the note, the mix box, the wire, the six right-column cards (the author's addition of 25 October); collapsed, a card shows its title row only; every state is remembered per viewer in local storage, with defaults by width (all cards open; the legend and note collapsed on a map under 600 px; the note collapsed at the national and regional zooms while the wire is open and expanded inside a city window, the viewer's own toggle winning).

**The header lock-up.** Three groups on one 44 px row that never touch: the striped title with the tagline under it at the left and no mark before it; one lock-up at the centre, the mark at the height of two text lines beside the name and affiliation in tracked small capitals over the dissertation title in the muted tone, centred as a unit on the header's width; the theme button at the right edge (round 11, item 8, the final form after two earlier arrangements: the byline at the right beside the button, then right-aligned next to it). Below 1,600 px the lock-up takes three lines, on phones it stacks under the title. The footer is one line: the author line with the mark, the email as selectable text with a copy button, the two square buttons to X, and at the right only the licences the data require.

**The two-sided legend (round 14, the author's request about the box's width).** The legend's key side, shown by default, holds the title and the rows with a swatch, so the box is as wide as its longest label; a green "i" in the title row, styled like the METHOD button, turns it to the reading side, the sentences on how to read the colours, wrapped to the key side's width; the choice is remembered per viewer. A group of rows that shares a phrase sits under a header that carries the phrase once (the listed marks: "On one of the five lists (treated)"). The rule behind it: a sentence never sets a panel's width. Rejected: wrapping the long rows in place, which grows the box upward into the map.

**The phone rules.** Below 900 px the page scrolls: the map a fixed-height block (62vh, 360 to 560 px), the cards stacked under it, the legend and note collapsible and collapsed by default, the GO TO card a button list in place of the minimap, the wire scrolling as on a desktop, the tile placed above the finger on a tap, one name shown on the tile, the header stacked. Where the floating tile has no clear place on a phone map, it docks along the map's top edge at the map's width, never over the zoom box, the scale bar or the strip, the point below it; a search on a map under 450 px tall lands the firm at 62 percent of the height (round 14, the author's decision of 25 October; the scale bar stays visible, rejected: setting it aside while the tile shows). Touch: drag, pinch, tap for values, double-tap to zoom, `touch-action: none` on the map alone so the page still scrolls from the panels (24 October).

## 6. Data honesty

**Every figure traceable.** Each number on screen comes from a checked pass with a provenance record (`motion/data/*.provenance.json`), and every string is a template over named fields of the shipped data; the wire's items are re-derived item by item by an independent checker that shares only the data with the generator (round 10, W14); the manifests carry their checks; no number computed in the lab is copied by hand into a page. The passes are deterministic and reproducible from pinned sources (188 entries in `config/sources.json`).

**Descriptive wording.** Absence from the archive is not withdrawal or concealment; the lists are fixed memberships, not dated designations; associations are descriptive, never causal ("Paths compared, no effect estimated."); the 2025 extension is flagged; no regional reading is put on the change map; disclosure rules stand (shares need 50 firms, list counts under 5 per cell are suppressed, fine-cell counts under 10 are banded). The chapter treats membership as fixed and undated, so the dated turning-red is its own descriptive layer, said so in the record (25 October).

**Identity never by colour alone.** The legend and the tile name every class; the residual colour pairs under the validator's floors are recorded with that naming as the secondary encoding; the listed states carry glyphs (solid, hollow, rimmed) and the listed firms their names; the ramps are checked for colour-vision separation and the accents for distance from the red; the chart series are labelled.

**The audit habit.** Nothing ships on a prediction: the atlas audit at 400, 768, 1,200, 1,600 and 2,000 px on both renderers must report zero findings (canvas text boxes, DOM boxes, cameras, the colour rules, the wire, the layouts of a hidden-frame load), the video's text audit zero plus the counted lines, the palette validator for every colour, frame costs before and after, frames to the author at every checkpoint, and the author's word before every publish and the final commit of a round.

## 7. Rejected options, with reasons

- **The news feed.** The author's list names it among the rejected options; the lab's records hold no entry of their own for it. The principle it would break is recorded: the wire carries short dated true statements from the shipped data, re-derived by a checker, with tips and one cat slot in fixed places; nothing on the page reads as news or as a live feed. The entry is left for the author to complete.
- **The left-bar buttons.** The 3 px left-edge bar inside a 1.5 px role border, proposed in the plan and shown in the chrome mock of 25 October; rejected the same day on every button and control, permanently; a full 3 px role border took its place.
- **The muted accents at rest.** Round 9's first palette rested the fuchsia and the green in muted variants and lit them on movement; corrected the same day: the full colours rest, the brighter live variants show only while something moves or has focus.
- **Dragging windows.** Rejected on 25 October for the mix box, the note and the legend: all three read, none acts; they collapse instead.
- **White body text.** Pure white never rests: the ink is #ECEEF1 and the muted tone one step below it; white appeared only as a live variant in a superseded proposal.
- **The Syne titles.** A second face for display type, offered as an experiment; dropped by the author on 25 October for one typeface.
- **A third accent.** Two accents only, each with a role; travel took the page's neutral (ink, then ice blue), not a third role; the one red control belongs to the red marks, not to the chrome.
- Also rejected along the way: the round-2 navy (the red under 3:1), the charcoal (superseded by the navy stage), YlGnBu and PRGn (replaced by the cyan ramp and the amber and cyan pair), ColorBrewer's qualitative nine (replaced by the author's lit hues), purple absent marks (not drawn), the pan-then-zoom flight (replaced by the directed zoom), the CSS keyframe wire (replaced by the queue), a 10 px minimap label (9 px fits NIZHNY NOVGOROD), the overlay-blend grain (invisible), a clipped corner (clips the focus ring), the "(designated)" gloss (four lists are undated), a St Petersburg sequence (no statistic beyond Moscow's band), and the cells-only public cuts (retired with the firm-level rule change of round 5).
