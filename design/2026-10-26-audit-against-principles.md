# Audit of the two pages and the video's panels against the design principles (26 October 2026)

Read against `design/principles.md` on 26 October 2026 (round 12, part B): the atlas page and script, the explainer page and its stylesheet, and the video's panels in the scene code. Each departure names the principle it breaks, the file and line, and a one-line fix; ordered by visibility, the most visible first. The lab fixes nothing here; the author picks.

1. **Semicolons on screen, in the wire and the tile.** Five wire facts join two statements with a semicolon ("…on the lists (treated); 195,010 firms with other geocodes", "…the largest coordinate pair holds N firms on no list; M firms at…", "2021: N firms at the largest coordinate pair; M at pairs shared by 100 or more…", "2024 against 2021: N supported 100 km cells; P% of located firms…", "N firms on the undated lists are red from 2014; M listing dates are inferred"), and the fine-cell tooltip adds "; share not shown under 50 firms". Principle: the wording standard, no em-dashes and no semicolons on screen (section 4). Where: `motion/js/explorer-app.js:1003`, `:1004`, `:1007`, `:1008`, `:1011` (the `add(…)` templates) and `:1364`; the checker's mirror templates in `motion/tools/wire_check.py`. Fix: a full stop or "and" in place of each semicolon, the same change in the checker.

2. **Two ambers in the Moscow section of the video.** Aeroflot's marker, header, line and dot take the anchor amber `--anchor-a` #F2B134 while the revenue classes around it, since round 10, take the diverging pair's amber #FFB000 and orange #F39523 for the declines. Principle: colour means one thing at a time; a colour that identifies a firm must not be a class colour in the same frame (section 2, the listed red's reservation and "what never takes colour"). Where: `motion/css/theme.css:24` (`--anchor-a`), `motion/js/treated-scenes.js:524` (`drawMarker`) and the profile headers, comparison lines and live box that read `theme.anchorA`. Fix: give Aeroflot a colour outside both ramps and the red, validated by the palette script against the pair (the ink #ECEEF1 with its circle marker is the nearest candidate), and keep the mint for the private firm.

3. **The comparison element's medians and bands in a muted teal.** `--accent-muted` #6FA39A, from round 3's charcoal palette, colours the group medians and the interquartile bands in the comparison beat and the live box. Principle: no muted accents at rest, and series take the ramp's family (section 2). Where: `motion/css/theme.css:24`, `motion/js/treated-scenes.js:367` and `:375` to `:376`. Fix: the cyan ramp's step 3 (#50B5AA) for the median line and step 2 at 0.22 for the band, the token retired.

4. **Links on the explainer page in the ramp's accent.** The Videos line's links take `--accent`, the cyan ramp's step 4, a data colour on interface text. Principle: two accents with roles, data colours never on chrome (section 2). Where: `motion/css/theme.css:104` (`.note a`). Fix: `color: var(--cursor)` (a link acts), the ink on hover; the atlas's `footer a` rule (`motion/explorer.html:316`) has the same value but is overridden by `.btn` on the only links there and shows nothing.

5. **The credits' caveat keeps "Supplied lists".** "Supplied lists are fixed memberships, not dated designations." is the one place the retired provenance word still reaches the screen, recorded on 24 October as the exception. Principle: the wording standard (section 4). Where: `motion/js/explainer-scenes.js:375`. Fix: "The lists are fixed memberships, not dated designations.", the atlas's own sentence; both cuts re-rendered (the credits change the last 7 s of each).

6. **The minimap's labels at 9 px.** Below the floors the system states elsewhere (11 px on the atlas canvases, 22 px in the video); recorded on 26 October as a deviation because 10 px does not fit NIZHNY NOVGOROD in the 100 px column. Principle: the text floors (sections 1 and 4). Where: `motion/explorer.html:273` (`.minimap text`). Fix: none without a wider label column (the minimap would need about 20 px more at the left, taking them from the 272 px outline box); or keep the recorded deviation.

7. **The cat prefix names a token that no longer exists.** `.wi.cat .pre { color: var(--context-muted); }` references the muted context colour removed with the "neon rests" correction, so the prefix renders in the full green; the stylesheet still asks for a muted accent at rest. Principle: no muted accents at rest (section 2; met on screen by accident). Where: `motion/explorer.html:176`. Fix: `color: var(--context)`, or a second tone for the prefix if the author wants the item 15 distinction back (the muted text tone #B4C0D0, not a muted accent).

Checked and clean: no rounded corner outside the round listed-state glyphs and the minimap's dots; no shadow, filter, blur or backdrop effect on either page; no tonal gradient (the slider track and the legend bars are hard-stop fills); no retired 600 weight on either page or in the video (the two matches in the scene code are positions, not weights); no em-dash; no "supplied list", "treatment group", "sanctioned or strategic" or "control" on screen beyond item 5; the video's panels are flat `fillRect` surfaces at 0.92 with no radius; the explainer's control buttons are tracked capitals and its chapter buttons mixed case by decision; the eyebrows and location lines are tracked capitals; the accents sit only on the roles the document names; "(treated)" appears nowhere in the video.
