# Round 36 (2 November 2026): Geist Mono in place of IBM Plex Mono

The author previewed the home page in Vercel's Geist Serif and Geist Mono on 2 November 2026 (`motion/build/geist-preview/`, the page `geist-preview.html`, built from scratch copies of the site by `scripts/` there, with each copy's diff in `diffs/`) and chose Geist Mono everywhere IBM Plex Mono was used: the site, the atlas, the film and the lab's review pages. Not the résumé (pdfLaTeX, Bitstream Charter) and not the Armenian note (DejaVu Sans Mono). The author's decisions of the same day: the pages serve the one variable file; the renders use the three static faces installed locally; Google Fonts is dropped from both publish copies; the local Plex install and its pins go once the film's re-render has passed; the two pointer pages at the old artifact addresses stay as published. The review page is `2026-11-02-round-36.html` (built by `2026-11-02-round-36-page.py` from the site's proof, the card's dry run and the atlas's proof).

## The faces

Geist Mono (Vercel, with basement.studio; SIL Open Font License 1.1), from the `geist` npm package 1.9.0 (tarball sha256 80c7cd136d43444d26a3d6aa425e0b957d1962993e76d97716731e7dc32f14c6; the files' sizes and hashes in `data/external/fonts/README.md`). Read from the font files' OS/2 and hhea tables:

| Face | Cap height | x-height | Ascent / descent | Advance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Geist Mono 400 / 500 / 700 | 710 | 530 / 532 / 536 | 1,005 / −295 | 600 |
| IBM Plex Mono 400 / 500 / 700 | 698 | 516 | 1,025 / −275 | 600 |

The same 0.6 em advance and the same 1.3 em line box, so no element moves; the baseline sits 0.02 em higher and the capitals 0.012 em taller, so glyphs sit 1 to 2 px higher in their boxes at display sizes and under 1 px at text sizes. The name table mirrors Plex's (the Medium's family "Geist Mono Medium", typographic family "Geist Mono"), so fontconfig resolves "Geist Mono" at weights 80, 100 and 200 to the three files as it did Plex's, and the audit's platform-font checks hold with the string swapped.

Glyph coverage, tested in the browser against every non-ASCII character in the site's files: Geist Mono draws all 63 Cyrillic letters and the four accented Latin ones the pages use; of the symbols it lacks the page titles' corner brackets, the non-breaking hyphen and one combining mark, which Plex lacked too, and it has ≤ and ≥, which Plex lacked. Neither has Armenian: the REFERENCES note's 'Atlas Armenian Mono' (DejaVu Sans Mono) stays.
