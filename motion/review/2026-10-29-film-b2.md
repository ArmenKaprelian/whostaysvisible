# The film, cut B2 (29 October 2026)

The author asked, while away, for two revised cuts of the film: a conservative one (A) and a bolder one (B). Both were built in a scratchpad copy of the explainer at 37c5520, in 720p, with nothing in the lab or on the site changed. The author picked B and asked for a design, alignment and spacing pass over it (B2). B2 was approved from before/after frames of the 12 scenes it changes and from its 720p cut. Both versions are applied here.

## Cut B: what it changed from the published film (287 s to 244 s)

- **Groups.** The slope charts gave way to the atlas's round-24 Groups table (`MotionCharts.changes`, with a `progress` option added for the film; at 1, its default, the atlas's card draws as before): joint-stock, private, state-related and state-run (from `data/statesplit.json`, which the page now loads). The sentence now reads "fell among state-related firms. The state-run firms are about half of them and carry the whole decline (70.1% to 48.8%)", followed by one muted definition line.
- **Plain labels (the author's rule of 29 October 2026).** "Q5 vs Q1" became "largest fifth" (with the 2nd, 3rd and 4th fifths), and the subtitle reads "against the smallest fifth by assets". "Fixed 2019 cohort" became "firms followed from 2019". The four 2021 cohorts became "Four groups fixed in 2021", with the rows "Revenue and cash", "Revenue and inventories", "Margin and cash" and "Margin and inventories". The atlas keeps "Q5 vs Q1" until the author takes the size labels up as a larger theme.
- **P-C.** "Provisional:" left the size note; its concrete sentence stays.
- **The pulse.** The film takes the atlas's swell (`MM.swellPhase`, `MM.swellPeak`), with no glow, pale core or rings, and keeps the west-to-east stagger. In the national run, 2024 (1,480 firms added to a list, the atlas's own mask) holds for 2.6 s while the atlas's sweep crosses the country by longitude in 1.5 s and the year's minimum spanning tree traces behind it. The caption uses the atlas's words: "1,480 FIRMS ADDED TO A LIST IN 2024" and "Lines join them by distance only."
- **One run instead of two.** The city and region runs became one pass of the years while the camera pulls out from the city scale (k 30) to the region (k 12) on an S-curve in log k (a logistic of steepness 12), so the 1 km and 5 km grids hand over in under 2 s around 2019. The ticker's place changes where k crosses 20, at 144.5 s.
- **Pacing.**
  - The beats: hook 9.5 s, archive 13, availability 13.5, years 18 (1.27 s a year), change 9.5, zoom 10, groups 13.5, transitions 10.5, size 14, lists 14, next 11, credits 7.5.
  - The two-firm sequence is 100 s instead of 120: the street zoom 11, the profiles 12, the designation 8, the comparison 13, the return 6, downtown 14, the merged run 12.5, national 13.5, the close 7, the pull-out 3.
  - The chart slides fade in and out in 0.35 s instead of 0.5.
- **The credits.** They end with "THE VISIBILITY ATLAS · armenkaprelian.github.io/atlas".

## B2: the spacing pass

- **The comparison.** The plots are 256 px tall instead of 290, one key line is centred under both, and each side's facts sit on a 780 px column with 1.45 leading, in boxes of equal height.

- **The availability chart's event labels** hang at the feet of their own lines (`eventLeaders`, `eventRowGap` 40 in `MotionCharts.lines`), with the rows ordered so that no line crosses another event's label.

Elsewhere:

- **The scale note** moved up to clear the legend.
