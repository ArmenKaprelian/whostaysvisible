# Round 20 (29 October 2026): the atlas's fonts from its own folder

The author's decision of 29 October 2026, made in answer to a question in a pass over the site (`~/Documents/website`): serve IBM Plex Mono from the site and from the Visibility Atlas, not from Google Fonts. Google's servers then see no visitor's address, and the page no longer waits on a stylesheet from another host.

## What changed

- `motion/fonts/`: the 19 woff2 files Google's stylesheet lists for `family=IBM+Plex+Mono:wght@400;500;700&family=Tomorrow:wght@400;500&display=swap` (Plex in five subsets, cyrillic-ext, cyrillic, vietnamese, latin-ext and latin, at three weights; Tomorrow in latin-ext and latin at two), renamed `FAMILY-WEIGHT-SUBSET.woff2`; `IBMPlexMono-OFL.txt` and `Tomorrow-OFL.txt` from `google/fonts` (`ofl/ibmplexmono/OFL.txt`, `ofl/tomorrow/OFL.txt`); `web-fonts.css`, Google's 19 rules with their unicode-range lines and the urls pointing to `fonts/`.
- `tools/publish-set.py`: `FONT_LOCAL` names the stylesheet the atlas's page gets inlined in place of the Google Fonts link; `page_fonts()` reads it, so the set ships the files it names; `licence()` maps a family's files to its licence. The explainer keeps the link (`FONT`), and `FONT_EXTRA` is gone with the atlas's Tomorrow parameter.
- Fetched once on 29 October 2026 with a generic User-Agent and no personal data: the stylesheet from fonts.googleapis.com, the files from fonts.gstatic.com, the licences from raw.githubusercontent.com.

## The publish, under the author's one-time exception

The exception covers this publish only: no five-width audit, the other steps of `publish.sh` by hand. The standing rule and `publish.sh` are unchanged. The checks in its place:

1. **Proof.** The lab's page does not change (the lab renders with the installed family), so a proof of HEAD against the working tree would be identical without testing anything. The proof compared the published copies instead: base, the site's live atlas served from `~/Documents/website` (Google Fonts); trial, the new set imported into `build/site-pass-2026-10-29/trial-atlas`. Result: identical, with no string at 1,600 or 400 px, none of 88 frames and no rule hit differing (260 s). Sheet: `build/site-pass-2026-10-29/proof-fonts/proof.html`.
2. **The set.** `publish-set.py` without `--write`: nothing refused, 21 files added (the 19 woff2 and 2 licences) and 2 changed (both pages' build line; the atlas's font block). Imported, the atlas differs from the live one in `index.html` (the build line and the font block) and the 21 new files, nothing else; the agent guide is unchanged (same UTC day).
3. **Both engines.** `site-check.py --site build/site-pass-2026-10-29/trial-atlas` (the site pass and the new atlas together, as they would go live): 34 page loads in Brave and WebKit, zero findings (42 s; `build/site-check/20261029T060225Z`). The font check passes in both engines, and the atlas loads at 390 and 1,440 px without errors or sideways scroll.
4. **The faces.** In the trial atlas: 20 faces (19 web fonts and DejaVu for Armenian), none in error; the woff2 files fetched are Plex's latin and cyrillic and Tomorrow's latin, all from `fonts/`; no request to fonts.googleapis.com or fonts.gstatic.com. The live atlas makes one request there.

The five-width audit was not run: none of the four checks failed or showed anything unexpected.

One note on the proof's base: Brave leaves third-party font files out of a page's resource list, so the live copy's woff2 requests to fonts.gstatic.com do not show there. Its faces did load from Google: Google's rules have no other source, and the Latin faces report "loaded".
