# Round 23 (29 October 2026): the swell at twice its size, REFERENCES at 98 percent

After round 22 went live the author found the swell not prominent enough and asked for REFERENCES one step more opaque.

## The swell

Previewed from a sandbox copy of the published build with `MM.swellPeak` scaled: stills at the peak (0.45 s) and real-time clips of the 2024 pulse (1,480 firms, the Firms view, the panels hidden) at Moscow and at the country, at 1x (2.5 to 6 px of extra radius), 1.5x (3.75 to 9 px) and 2x (5 to 12 px). The note sent with them: at 2x central Moscow becomes one red mass at the peak, and the hollow marks (listed, no statement that year) swell as open circles that begin to read as small rings. The author picked 2x.

The build sets `M.swellPeak = r => Math.min(12, Math.max(5, 2.4 * r))`. Of ten frames (150 to 900 ms, both views), nine match the 2x preview pixel for pixel and one differs in 2 pixels (rounding: the preview computed 2 x 1.2 r). Script time per pulse frame in headless Brave: about 2.4 ms at the country and 1.5 ms at Moscow, as at 1x. The Cells view's dot takes the same peak and doubles: at Moscow's 1 km squares it nearly fills its square at the peak, inside it; frames of it at 0.3 s on both themes went to the author with the report.

## REFERENCES

`REFS_ALPHA` 98, with the CSS fallback to match; the author's pick from round 22's corrected sheet (94 to 99 percent).
