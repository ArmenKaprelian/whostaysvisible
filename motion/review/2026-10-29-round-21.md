# Round 21 (29 October 2026): the address files fetched as gzipped binaries

The author's pick C of 29 October 2026. Measured on local copies before the change, as the author asked: the transfer size as GitHub Pages serves the files, the year-switch decode time in the author's Brave, and the memory.

## Transfer size

GitHub Pages answered gzip when brotli was offered (pages.github.com, 29 October 2026), so gzip is the comparison. The `.bin.gz` is served as `application/gzip` (Python's server here gave the same type) and needs no further compression.

The twin is base64 of the already gzipped file, so gzip takes it back to within 0.5 percent of the binary.

## Decode time (the author's Brave)

`motion/build/bench-firms/index.html`: path A injects the twin, `atob`s it, copies it byte by byte and gunzips it with `DecompressionStream`; path B fetches the `.bin.gz` and gunzips it the same way. Served from localhost with no-store. In the author's Brave 156 through the Claude extension on 29 October 2026, the laptop screen (devicePixelRatio 1.33), the window focused and visible for every run (none lost), five runs per path and year, alternating. Medians in ms:

| Year | A total | A script | A atob | A copy | A gunzip | B total | B fetch | B gunzip |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2014 | 303.5 | 104.6 | 25.0 | 29.0 | 147.0 | 205.4 | 29.8 | 173.7 |
| 2021 | 303.2 | 107.5 | 24.1 | 27.7 | 130.4 | 203.6 | 30.4 | 173.2 |
| 2025 | 304.3 | 104.4 | 19.7 | 34.5 | 132.6 | 201.2 | 28.5 | 170.7 |

Headless Brave (regressions only): A about 150 ms, B about 125 ms. On the live site the download of about 6.5 MB comes on top of either path.

## Identity

For all twelve years the decoded bytes have one SHA-256 and one length through both browser paths and through Python's decode of both files, and the twin's base64 decodes to the `.bin.gz` byte for byte.
