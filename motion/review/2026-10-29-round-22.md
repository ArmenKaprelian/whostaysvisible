# Round 22 (29 October 2026): the swell pulse, REFERENCES at 98 percent, the crosshair retired

The author's requests and picks of 29 October 2026, made from sandbox previews (the lab's working files untouched until the picks): the previews were patched copies of the atlas in Claude's scratchpad, served on their own ports, so nothing in `motion/` changed before the build.

## The pulse

The author found the round-15 pulse too close to an explosion and saw it stutter. That pulse faded each newly listed red mark in over 0.35 s while one thin red ring grew to 44 to 64 px and faded; in 2024 that is 1,480 rings at once, staggered west to east.

Three alternatives were previewed as real-time clips at Moscow and at the country (2024, the Firms view, the panels hidden), against the current pulse:

- **A, Swell** (the author's own idea; picked): each red mark grows by `min(6, max(2.5, 1.2 r))` px and settles back over 0.9 s (a half sine), its red fading in over 0.2 s; no ring, no glow, no pale core.
- **B, Settle:** a ring closes from 12 px outside the mark onto it over 0.7 s while the mark turns red.
- **C, Ping:** the current ring cut to 12 px of reach and 1.25 px of stroke, over 0.9 s.

The round-15 record rejected "the pulse's glow, swell and pale core (the explosion)". That swell was the bloom's flash, up to 14 px with a pale core and a glow; A is pure red and reaches less than half of it. The conflict was named to the author with the previews before the pick.

Frame pacing while a pulse plays (headless Brave at 1,600 by 1,000, no GPU, three runs each; the speed rule's measurement in the author's Brave was not made):

| | current | A Swell | B Settle | C Ping |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| replay button, frames over 25 ms in 2 s | 29 to 41 of 72 to 91 | 0 to 1 of 120 | 2 to 4 of about 117 | 2 to 4 of about 117 |
| forward year change, frames over 25 ms | about 31 | 1 to 3 | 5 | 5 to 6 |

The script time of a pulse frame was small in every variant (2 to 4 ms at the country, under 2 ms at Moscow, against 1 to 3 ms for a redraw at rest), so the stutter came from painting the large rings, not from the redraw's code. Every year change also has one frame of 150 to 183 ms in every variant: the year switch itself, not the pulse.

The build: `MM.swellPhase`, `MM.swellPeak` and `MM.swellDot` in `js/map-layers.js`; `drawMarks` draws the listed marks in one pass (the glow pass and the ring pass above the names are gone); the Cells view's pulse draws a red dot at each pulsing cell's centre that swells and goes (`cellPulseDots`, formerly `cellPulseRings`), a counterpart chosen in the build and shown to the author at 0.3 s. The author then asked for a casing under the dot (on the plain red dot the orange Construction and the amber cells swallowed it): a 1.5 px stroke of the stage colour outside the dot, drawn before the fill, as the hover ring is cased. Shown on both themes; on the light theme the near-white stage made it a white ring, and the author first kept it to the dark theme. Then, since on the light theme the listed red is only 1.01 to 1.62:1 against every industry colour (Trade 1.02, Agriculture 1.01), so the dots are told from their cells by hue alone, the author asked for the casing there too at 1 px, shown at 0.3 s at Moscow and picked. A side chat's suggestion of an ink hairline at 60 percent for the light theme was not taken (the author's instruction). Under reduced motion the marks, and the Cells view's dots, are held at 0.6 of the swell for 1.8 s, with no pale core (round 15's still highlight carried one). `MM.pulsePhase` and `MM.pulseRing` stay for the ring of the firm found by name and for the film's code, which keeps its own bloom. The pulse still lasts 1.8 s (the audit's live-state checks read it); the swell is over after 0.9 s plus the stagger.

The real build's swell matches the approved clips: of ten frames (150 to 900 ms, both views) nine are identical pixel for pixel; the tenth, the first frame taken after the page loaded, differs only where the opening sweep had not yet drawn the ordinary dots.

## The REFERENCES panel

The stage under the panel at 97 percent, from 94: `REFS_ALPHA` in `js/explorer-app.js`, which sets `--refs-alpha` on the dialog's own style, and the CSS fallback in `explorer.html` to match. The first previews (96 to 99 percent) set `--refs-alpha` on the root; the dialog's inline value overrode it, so inside the panel every one of them was pixel-identical to 94, and the author's first pick (98) was made from them. Found when the proof's REFERENCES frame came out identical to HEAD's. The corrected previews patched the constant (inside the panel 93 to 96 percent of the pixels differ from 94), and the author picked 97 from them. A preview of a token should be checked for a change inside the element it is meant to change before it is shown.

## The crosshair

The two 2 px fuchsia lines that followed the cursor across the map are retired, on the author's request; the hover ring, the region outline and the tile are unchanged, and the hover ring keeps the live and resting colours. The code and its behaviour are kept for reuse in `2026-10-29-crosshair-retired.md`; the last commit with it is `f895dcd`. Removing it also removes the 120 ms easing loop that redrew the overlay on every pointer move.
