## Why the published layout left the space

The headshot's width was derived from the height left above the index's band (`--shot`: 100dvh − band − 140 px, at most half the column), so on a tall window it was large, while the bio stayed at 16 and 13 px: on the author's window the bio ended 158 px above the photo's bottom, on a 2,560 × 1,440 desktop 246 px. On short windows the opposite: the photo at its 132 px floor and the bio 62 to 84 px taller than it. The band reserved a fixed height for the top block (330 px, 352 px under 1,500 wide and 860 tall) and a small-laptop rule tuned the headshot and the dissertation's line to that reservation: a wording change could overflow it by a few px, as happened in round 32.

## The rule

1. The headshot's width stays as it was (the formula above, the 22 px rounding dropped).
2. The bio's type is proportional to that width in the split layout (`@media (min-width: 1200px) and (min-height: 600px)`): `--f: clamp(16px, var(--shot) * .062, 26px)` for the statement, `--g: clamp(13px, var(--shot) * .05, 21px)` for the credentials, line pitches 1.375 and 1.6923 (22 px at the floors), the gap between them 1.375 em, the capitals' shift −0.25 em.
3. The bio's measure: `max-width: max(var(--shot) * 1.4, (258px - var(--shot)) * 10000)`: 1.4 headshots (37 characters) while the type is proportional, so the line count and the bio's height stay in step with the headshot on a wide column; under a 258 px headshot the type is at its floor and the second term frees the measure.
4. The headshot's frame is as tall as the bio: `height: 100%; min-height: var(--shot); max-height: calc(var(--shot) * 1.6); object-fit: cover` (the phone card's mechanism, now on every layout).
5. The band yields: `.content { height: 100dvh; grid-template-rows: 1fr minmax(0, var(--band)) }` with `--band: var(--photo)` (35cqw); a definite height is what lets the second row give room to the first. The reservations and the small-laptop rule are deleted; `.content .top` keeps 16 px under the bio (the check's rule asks 8), and the 140 px the headshot formula leaves keeps the band whole on windows that have the room (the stacked layouts set `height: auto`).

Measured on the proposal: the bio ends within 6 px of the headshot's bottom at every window but 1280 × 720 (27 px below a 132 × 211 crop); the page never exceeds its window; the index's rule sits on the photo's top edge from 1440 × 900 up and 28 to 178 px below it on the short windows (the author's pick A of round 32), with the rows compressed to 47 to 66 px there.
