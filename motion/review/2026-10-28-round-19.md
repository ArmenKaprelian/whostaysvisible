## WebKit fixes (the author's iPhone/WebKit pass)

Read for: APIs Safari lacks or added late, canvas and WebGL quirks, touch and pinch, the Firms view's memory on phones.

Fixed:
- Safari's trackpad pinch (`js/explorer-app.js`, after the wheel handler): `gesturestart`, `gesturechange` and `gestureend` are cancelled, and the map zooms by each change in scale about the pointer when no finger is down. A Ctrl-wheel during a gesture is ignored. Tested with synthetic events: a scale of 1.5 zooms the map by 1.5, all four events cancelled.
- iOS zooms into a focused field under 16 px: `@supports (-webkit-touch-callout: none)` sets the FIND field and the link field to 16 px on iOS only.
- The bottom padding clears the home indicator: `padding-bottom: max(10px, env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`.
- `-webkit-text-size-adjust: 100%` keeps text size when an iPhone turns to landscape.

Judged and left alone:
- `DecompressionStream` (Safari 16.4): the Firms view is already off without it (`FIRMS_OK`).
- `requestIdleCallback` (absent in Safari): already falls back to a timer.
- WebGL2 float blending: the field already falls back to the 2D renderer.
- `<dialog>` (Safari 15.4) and `color-mix()` (16.2): only iOS releases before those, on devices that can update.
- Canvas `letterSpacing`: guarded; without it labels are untracked, not broken.
- The Firms view's memory: at most three years decoded, each year's base64 text dropped after decoding, older years and their GPU buffers freed.
- Canvases at the device pixel ratio of 3: well under iOS's canvas memory cap by estimate; not measured on a phone.
