## The Groups card: what went wrong

The data were right. The four shares come from the chapter's source files (`ch2-p2-addendum-ownership-source.csv`, `-legal-source.csv`); each is statements over eligible firms, recomputed from its numerator and denominator; the groups' denominators add up to the national eligible counts exactly (3,357,109 in 2021, 3,147,504 in 2024) in both classifications; `series.checks.csv` matches every value to the chapter's.

The layout was wrong. `MotionCharts.slopes` with `spread: true` pushed each column's labels apart to one line of text, in order, from the top. The 2021 values (73.0, 69.8, 69.2, 64.2) span 8.8 points, about 18 px in the 124 px card, where four labels need 42 px; the push moved Private's label 24 px and Unitary's 20 px below their dots in the desktop card, 16 px on the phone and 11 px in the pop-out, so "Unitary 69.2%" sat beside Private's dot. A second fault held at every size: State-related and Unitary share one colour and start at one dot (69.8 and 69.2), and the 2024 ends carried no names. The principles require the chart series to be labelled.

Elsewhere: a probe recorded every call to `MotionCharts.spread`. The national chart's end labels move 6 px at most and carry their own names and colours; the odds chart's do not move. No other chart names its series by position alone.

## The pulse previews (sandbox only, the build unchanged)

From a copy of the build in the scratchpad, `?pv=` switching: A the 4x swell as built; B tree lines; C meridian sweep; D the swell front with no line (the side chat's addition). B to D sweep by longitude: the marks' km coordinates are Lambert azimuthal equal-area about 60° N, 100° E, so a firm's longitude is the inverse projection (Moscow comes out at 37.61° E) and C's line is a true meridian, drawn by the forward projection over the view's latitude range in 64 steps. The stagger is stretched to 1.5 s (60 to 200 ms now): the pulse lasts 2.4 s, not 1.8.
